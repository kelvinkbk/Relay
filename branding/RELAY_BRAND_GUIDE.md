# Relay Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity
**Relay** is the native communication and messaging application for **TheonixOS**.
The brand communicates connection, speed, flow, trust, modern technology, and human interaction.

## 2. Logo Concept & Symbol Construction
The primary symbol is an abstract "R" representing a continuous communication route between two connected nodes. 
- **Nodes**: Two dots (top left, bottom left) representing users or devices.
- **Path**: A flowing line connecting them, featuring a diagonal movement that implies forwarding, speed, and continuation.
- **Negative Space**: Subtly forms an "R".

### Geometry
- **Rounded geometry & smooth curves**
- **Consistent stroke weight**: Uses a thick, uniform stroke.
- **Minimalist**: Readable even at 16x16 pixels.
- **No Telegram Elements**: No paper planes, no arrows inside circles, no Telegram blue.

## 3. Typography
**Relay Wordmark**: Uses clean, modern typography (e.g., Inter, Outfit, or Roboto) with subtle customizations on the 'R' and 'y' to match the symbol's geometric curves.
- **TheonixOS Lockup**: "Relay - Messaging for TheonixOS" or "Relay by TheonixOS". This is strictly for marketing/About screens, not the primary UI.

## 4. Color Palette
A distinctive, cool-spectrum identity emphasizing modern technology and avoiding legacy blues.

### Primary Colors
- **Relay Primary**: `#6366F1` (Indigo)
- **Relay Accent**: `#8B5CF6` (Violet)

### Light Theme
- **Background Light**: `#F8FAFC`
- **Surface Light**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Primary Text Light**: `#0F172A`
- **Secondary Text Light**: `#64748B`

### Dark Theme
- **Background Dark**: `#0F172A`
- **Surface Dark**: `#1E293B`
- **Primary Text Dark**: `#F8FAFC`
- **Secondary Text Dark**: `#94A3B8`

## 5. Icon System
- **Corner Treatment**: Rounded squircle for the app icon container, matching modern desktop standards (e.g., macOS / TheonixOS).
- **Navigation Icons**: Outline style, 2px stroke, 24x24px grid. Minimalist and readable. Use Relay Accent for active states.
- **Status Icons**: extremely simple and recognizable (e.g., single check for sent, double check for read).

## 6. Do's and Don'ts
- **DO** use the monochrome symbol for the system tray and notifications.
- **DO** maintain clear space around the logo (at least 25% of the symbol's width).
- **DON'T** use a heavy gradient; keep colors flat or use an extremely subtle gradient.
- **DON'T** add drop shadows or 3D effects to the primary symbol.
- **DON'T** use the Telegram paper plane or any visual tropes associated with competitors.
